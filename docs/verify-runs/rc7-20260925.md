## What failed on the way, and why none of it is the product

1. **The AI providers were both down for the run.** Anthropic answered
   *"You have reached your specified API usage limits"* (the account's
   spend cap). Every classification then fell to Gemini, whose **free daily
   quota** ran out part-way through; its last refusal read "model
   `gemini-2.5-flash-lite` is no longer available", which was only the last
   model of the discovery walk, not a pinned setting. With nothing
   classifying, a greeting became a ticket and replies opened new ones —
   18 failures across three suites from one cause. Production was unaffected
   (a greeting to TFML's WhatsApp got a reply and opened no request).
   `lib/llm.ts` now logs every model's refusal, not only the last (`1e073b9`).
   ⚠️ Open: the production Gemini key should move to a paid tier — a free
   quota is a fallback that runs out exactly when it is needed.
2. **`verify-gateway-isolation` wanted distinct WhatsApp keys.** 5.5 had
   neutralised staging's and dev's routes with `outbound_token` NULL; this
   suite requires TFML and OEA to hold different non-null keys. Staging and
   dev now carry unique **placeholder** keys (`staging-placeholder-no-send-…`)
   that 360dialog rejects: the tests see distinct keys, and still nothing can
   message a real customer from the business numbers.
3. **Two infrastructure hiccups** — a dropped database connection and a
   300 s timeout — passed unchanged on re-run.
